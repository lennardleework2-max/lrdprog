Buyer Payments: pick a design

All three use the same sample buyers (Customer A matches the client PDF) and the same balance rules. Everything is in memory: reload or press Reset to start over.

Design 1 · Buyer Payment Dashboard

KPI cards + status bar + buyer table. A row opens a side drawer; Add Payment is a modal.

Strengths
  • Whole picture at a glance
  • Fast to find who owes most
  • Closest to the existing LRD list pages
Weaknesses
  • Running ledger isn't shown (drawer only)
  • More clicks to record many payments
Best for

Managers and owners checking overall collection status.

Design 2 · Customer Ledger Workspace

Buyer list on the left and a PDF-style statement on the right, with tabs for Ledger / DR / Payments / Cheques. Inline payment form.

Strengths
  • Matches the client's PDF
  • Full debit/credit running balance
  • Print Ledger + CSV preview
Weaknesses
  • Dense on phones (wide ledger table scrolls sideways)
  • One buyer at a time
Best for

Accounting staff reviewing or printing a customer's account.

Design 3 · Collections Workspace

Buyer cards sorted by balance + a quick-pay panel. Cheque Monitor board (Hold → For Deposit → Cleared / Returned) and an activity log.

Strengths
  • Fewest clicks per payment (quick-fill amounts)
  • Cheque follow-up across all buyers
  • Best on mobile (bottom tabs)
Weaknesses
  • No running ledger / print view
  • Less like current LRD screens
Best for

Collectors and cashiers recording payments throughout the day.

Open Design 3
Shared balance rules (same as the PDF)
  • Collected = Cash / GCash / Bank payments + Cleared cheques.
  • Cheques on Hand = Hold + For Deposit (not counted as collected).
  • Returned = Bounced + Closed Account (never reduces the debt).
  • Outstanding = Sales − Collected · Net = Sales − Collected − Cheques on Hand.
  • Status: Paid Partial Unpaid are based on collected amounts only.
